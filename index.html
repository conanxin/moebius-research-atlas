About the project

Evidence-based

Every claim is grounded in a published scan, a verified reproduction, or a documented observation. Evidence tiers are explicit: original artwork, publication scan, reproduction page, catalog render, research board, or text-only ledger entry.

Counterexample-driven

Hypotheses are tested against cases that should break them. A counterexample does not invalidate a hypothesis — it constrains the hypothesis's scope. The archive shows both passing and failing tests.

Open-source

Code is MIT-licensed. Research data is CC BY 4.0. Visual assets are attributed per item. The archive is reproducible: cloning the repository gives you the same HTML pages.

Open project

Source code, public research data, methodology, contribution guidelines and licenses — all open and version-controlled.
