03 · Master Model

Stable Spatial Hand × Task-Conditioned Information Regime

STATUS: SUPPORTED_WITH_LIMITS

What the limits are: the model has been tested against cross-decade counterexamples but is bounded by the current corpus. Status has not been promoted to "established" or "proven". See Open Questions for what is not yet established.

Comparing the 1970s formation works (§02) and the 1980s project-conditioned variety (§03) reveals two regularities. One — what stays roughly the same across all of Mœbius's work: control of contour, spatial readability, and physical legibility of strange forms. Two — what changes per project: how much text appears, how much the world explains itself, how clearly one event causes the next. The first is the Stable Spatial Hand. The second is the Task-Conditioned Information Regime.

Stable Spatial Hand

  1. Contour / silhouette control
  2. Spatial readability
  3. Physical legibility of strange forms

Resources (not invariants):

  • Scale shift
  • Deep staging

Task-Conditioned Information Regime

TEXT_AMOUNT
How much verbal information the work gives.
WORLD_EXPLANATION
How much the world explains itself.
EVENT_CAUSALITY
How clearly one event leads to the next.
FUNCTIONAL_INFORMATION
How much information helps the reader understand what things do.
VISUAL_AUTONOMY
How much the sequence can operate without verbal explanation.
PANEL_DENSITY
How compressed or spacious the page structure is.

Regime label examples:

  • Arzach — LOW_EXPLANATION / HIGH_VISUAL_CAUSALITY
  • The Long Tomorrow — HIGH_FUNCTIONAL / HIGH_CAUSALITY
  • Garage Hermétique — VARIABLE / GENERATIVE
  • Le Cœur couronné — HIGH_PROTOCOL
  • Stel — VARIABLE (internal HIGH_PROTOCOL ↔ LOW_EXPLANATION)
  • 40 Days — LOW_CAUSALITY_HIGH_VISUAL_AUTONOMY (candidate)
  • Fifth Element design — UNCLASSIFIED (non-narrative design pages)

07 · Le Cœur couronné — The Body Acts Before Explanation Arrives

Three volumes. One observation: events frequently happen to bodies first and are interpreted afterward.

Vol.1 · 1992

Body arc: PUBLIC_SOCIAL → PRIVATE_EXISTENTIAL → SACRED_EROTIC_GROTESQUE.

Vol.2 · 1993

Body arc: PUBLIC_MATERIAL_SOCIAL → INTOXICATED_DESIRED_REPRESSED → ENDANGERED_REPRODUCTIVE_BIOLOGICAL.

Vol.3 · 1998

Body arc: PUBLICLY_REPRESENTED → INTIMATE_EXPOSED → ENDANGERED_PHYSIOLOGICAL. Events happen to bodies first, interpreted afterward.

LANGUAGE ATTEMPTS CLOSURE → image / narrative continues → MEANING OPENS AGAIN.

This is not "words are false, images are true". It is a structural observation across three volumes.

08 · Counterexample · Stel vs 40 Days

Body visibility is not narrative causality.

Stel MEETS_THRESHOLD

World of Edena Ch.4 (2016 reprint).

B3_TRIGGER (fall/daze onset alters beast interaction; journey resumes)

B4_ENGINE (LATE: instrument invasion + neurological intervention → subconscious voyage)

40 Days DOES_NOT_MEET_THRESHOLD

40 Jours dans le Désert B (1999).

Body highly visible — but no clear cases where bodily state triggers next-page narrative condition.

CE_BODY_1 = PRESENT_STRONG · CE_BODY_3 = PRESENT_STRONG

PHASE6B_DECISION = PARTIAL_1_OF_2 · FOURTH_LAYER_CANDIDATE_STATUS = NOT_READY

One of the two non-Cœur projects met the frozen threshold; the other became a strong counterexample. The result is partial support, not failure — and not a full pass. Phase 6B is not promoted to "established", and a fourth model layer is not created.

Body visibility does not automatically equal narrative causality. Counterexamples can limit a model — that is research progress, not failure.

09 · How the Research Works

  1. IDENTITY GATE — verify a work's identity before using it as evidence
  2. SAMPLE FREEZE — rule-based continuous sequence; record SHA256 before browsing
  3. FRESH VISION — external reviewer sees pages without model vocabulary
  4. LEDGER — record observations with no rewrites after the fact
  5. HYPOTHESIS — formal claim; second-pass comparison against model
  6. COUNTEREXAMPLE — collect cases that constrain the hypothesis
  7. LOCK — when verdict is reached, freeze with documented limits

Three Method Lessons (real failures)

Case 01 · keyword-clean ≠ hypothesis-blind

A bundle passed the literal-contamination check but its README contained semantic hypothesis leakage ("especially notice body-state…", "look for narrative engine"). External reviewer saw the hypothesis embedded in the framing.

Outcome: bundle rebuilt with neutral questions only; anti-contamination upgraded to semantic check.

Case 02 · programmatically contiguous ≠ narratively contiguous

Phase 6B's first sample freeze selected pages by scan index; one included front matter, a chapter title page, and Moebius's prose preface. The pages were sequentially adjacent in the source scan, but they were not story.

Outcome: corpus boundary re-verified by external page-role adjudication; refreeze ran on the corrected story span.

Case 03 · confirmed work identity ≠ canonical visual asset

A work's identity was confirmed at SOURCE_IDENTIFIED level, but the linked local file turned out to be an edition / package page or a product montage, not an individual plate. The work was real; the asset role was wrong.

Outcome: asset_role must be adjudicated per file, not inherited from the work record. (See ASSET_METADATA_SCHEMA.)
