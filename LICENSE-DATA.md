## What this covers

- `data/public/atlas-works.json` — minimal metadata of works referenced by the Research Atlas
- `data/public/atlas-claims.json` — claim titles, statements, statuses, scopes, limitations
- `data/public/atlas-evidence.json` — claim → work → asset mapping
- `data/public/public-assets.json` / `public-assets.csv` — public visual asset list (paths, sha256, lineage)
- `docs/RESEARCH_METHOD.md` · `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` · `docs/EVIDENCE_MODEL.md` · `docs/PROJECT_STATUS.md` — research protocol documents
- Generated metadata such as claim-evidence structures, frozen ledgers (in their published form), and statement of model status.
